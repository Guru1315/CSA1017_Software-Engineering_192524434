## The case study

Design a **Predictive Maintenance Platform** for manufacturing. Factories lose time and money when machines such as CNC machines, conveyors, pumps and robotic arms stop without warning. The platform watches the condition of equipment across plants, learns what a healthy machine looks like, spots early signs of failure and schedules maintenance before the machine breaks, instead of fixing it afterwards or servicing it on a fixed calendar.
