## What it covers

Agile planning for the **Predictive Maintenance Platform** from [Assessment Tool 1](../ASSESSMENT%20TOOL%201/README.md). The vision is a secure, reliable and scalable platform that brings equipment health, maintenance history, preventive schedules and failure-risk indicators into one place, so teams can act before failures happen.
