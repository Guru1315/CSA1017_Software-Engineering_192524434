## What it shows

A mobile app flow of five screens, laid out left to right on the Figma canvas:

| # | Screen | What is on it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Home | From city and To city, travel date and a **Search Buses** button |
| 2 | Bus List | Three buses for Chennai to Bengaluru with times and prices (₹850, ₹720 and ₹990) and a **Book Now** button on each |
| 3 | Seat Selection | A seat grid marked available, selected or booked, with seats 14 and 15 selected (total ₹1,700) and a **Proceed to Payment** button |
| 4 | Payment | A ticket summary, a payment method dropdown (UPI) and a **Pay Now** button |
| 5 | Confirmation | A "Booking Confirmed" message with the ticket details and a **Download Ticket** button |

The layers panel on the left shows each screen as its own frame, and the Home screen's fields and button are named layers.

The prices, routes and ticket details in the prototype are sample content, not a real booking.
